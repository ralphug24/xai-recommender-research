# Study plan and methodological reflection

This document summarizes the final HCC 8330 proposal for portfolio readers. It is a later explanatory guide; the [original manuscript](../paper/final-proposal.pdf) remains the source for the course submission.

## Motivation and hypotheses

The project asks whether explanation features change the experience of receiving movie recommendations. The proposal's null hypothesis anticipates no difference between the compared conditions; its alternative hypothesis anticipates increased satisfaction and trust with explanation features.

The related work considers textual, visual and interactive explanations, user expertise and control over recommendations. The literature motivated examining both the explanation's presentation and the user's prior experience.

## Factors and outcomes

| Component | Proposal description |
| --- | --- |
| Explanation features | Textual explanations and visual or interactive features |
| Prior experience | Familiarity or experience with recommender systems |
| Satisfaction | Questionnaire judgments about the experience; the manuscript also discusses the System Usability Scale |
| Trust | Five-point agreement ratings |
| Recommendation quality | User judgments about the recommendations |
| Feature awareness and use | Questions about noticed and used recommendation features |

The questionnaire measures perceptions. A separate evaluation would be needed to establish objective recommendation accuracy. Usability, satisfaction and trust also require distinct definitions and suitable measures.

## Proposed participant journey

The manuscript proposed a six-month study with a recruitment target of 308 U.S. adults in two groups of 154. These are proposed targets, not completed recruitment or sessions.

1. **Ethics preparation:** prepare protocol, recruitment and consent materials before recruitment and obtain the required review.
2. **Baseline:** ask about platform use, familiarity with features and understanding of recommendations.
3. **Condition assignment:** specify exposure to explanation features and the role of prior experience in assignment or analysis.
4. **Interaction:** obtain movie recommendations using the proposed platforms and study conditions.
5. **Questionnaire:** gather judgments of satisfaction, trust, relevance, feature use, understanding and privacy perceptions.
6. **Analysis:** compare responses by condition and experience, and examine categorical feature-awareness/use associations.

The course submission included draft ethics materials. Their preparation does not indicate approval of a participant study.

## Analysis in the original paper

The proposal describes categorical comparisons using a chi-square test and mosaic plots. Its expected-results examples illustrate an analysis approach. They are not empirical findings from a conducted study.

Associations among feature-awareness categories would answer a different question from a causal comparison of explanation formats. An implementation should specify those questions and their analyses separately.

## What I would refine before recruitment

### Isolate the explanation

Comparing Netflix and MovieLens introduces differences in interfaces, content and recommendation algorithms. A more controlled comparison would hold the recommendation list and interface constant and vary the explanation. Prior experience could be recorded separately rather than conflated with platform assignment.

### Match each measure to its claim

Define satisfaction, trust, understanding and perceived relevance separately. Select suitable measures, pilot the wording and use consistent scoring directions. Evaluate objective recommendation performance separately if the research question includes model accuracy.

### Specify a consistent protocol

The manuscript describes a between-subjects design alongside descriptions of both-platform use. Before collecting data, reconcile the assignment and exposure rules, choose a consistent participation schedule, justify sample size for the final design and specify the analysis plan.

## Research takeaway

The project developed research framing, literature synthesis, experimental planning and collaborative documentation. Its main methodological lesson is to maintain a clear link between the question, the comparison, the measurement and the conclusions the evidence can support.
